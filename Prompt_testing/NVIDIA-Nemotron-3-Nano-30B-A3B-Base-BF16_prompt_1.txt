- Fillers/q‑s (e.g., dicalcium phosphate) – balance to 400 mg  

**Manufacturing flow**

1. **IR core** – blend API with PEG 400, dry, compress into ~100 mg cores.  
2. **SR granules** – dry‑blend polymers, pore‑former, erosion agent, plasticizer, etc.; add a slight PVP solution to form slug‑granulate; dry, mill, add lubricant.  
3. **Tablet press** – place IR core on lower punch, fill with SR granules, compress with upper punch → ~500 mg tablet (core + matrix).  
4. (Optional) Light film coat (Eudragit L‑100‑55, ≤ 5 % of tablet weight) if taste‑masking is needed.

**Why it works**

- The IR core dissolves instantly, delivering the first 15 % dose within ≈ 2 h.  
- The outer matrix uses a high‑viscosity HPMC blend (K4M + K100M) plus a small amount of water‑impermeable Eudragit RS 30D and a modest erosion agent (SSG). This gives an **anomalous (n≈0.55‑0.65) transport** that releases the remaining drug linearly, reaching ~85 % cumulative release by 8 h.

**Key take‑away:**  
- **Core + matrix** approach with a burst core and a polymer blend that balances diffusion and erosion reproduces the desired release profile in a single tablet.

Fine-tuned model answer:
**Sustained-Release Tablet Formulation (~500 mg)**
*Target profile: 2h–15% / 4h–40% / 6h–60% / 8h–85%*

---

**Composition**

| Component | Function | % w/w | mg/tablet |
|-----------|----------|-------|-----------|
| API | Active ingredient | 25% | 125 |
| HPMC K100M | High-viscosity gel former (slows release) | 30% | 150 |
| HPMC K4M | Medium-viscosity gel former (fine-tunes release) | 15% | 75 |
| Ethylcellulose (10 cps) | Hydrophobic retardant (adds tortuosity) | 10% | 50 |
| Lactose monohydrate | Soluble filler / pore-former | 12% | 60 |
| MCC (Avicel PH 102) | Binder / compressibility aid | 5.5% | 27.5 |
| Sodium starch glycolate | Erosion promoter (prevents gel stagnation) | 1.5% | 7.5 |
| PVP K-30 | Granulation binder | 0.5% | 2.5 |
| Talc | Glidant | 0.5% | 2.5 |
| Magnesium stearate | Lubricant | 0.5% | 2.5 |

---

**Manufacturing Process**

1. **Dry mixing** – Blend API, both HPMC grades, ethylcellulose, lactose, MCC, and sodium starch glycolate for 15 min.
2. **Wet granulation** – Add PVP K-30 solution (10% in isopropanol) to form wet mass; granulate; dry at 45–50°C.
3. **Milling** – Pass dried granules through 1.0 mm screen.
4. **Lubrication** – Blend with talc and magnesium stearate for 5 min.
5. **Compression** – Compress into tablets using 10 mm tooling, target hardness 10–20 kP.

---

**How It Works**

- **HPMC K100M + K4M** form a cohesive hydrogel layer upon contact with dissolution fluid, controlling drug diffusion. The high-viscosity grade ensures prolonged retention, while K4M fine-tunes the release slope.
- **Ethylcellulose** – as an insoluble polymer – increases matrix tortuosity, slowing water penetration and drug egress, especially during the initial phase.
- **Sodium starch glycolate** acts as a mild erosion agent, preventing the gel layer from becoming too thick and impermeable in later hours, enabling the 85% release target by 8h.
- **Lactose** dissolves out, leaving micropores that facilitate controlled drug release without compromising matrix integrity.
- **MCC** provides mechanical strength and good compressibility.

**Expected Release Mechanism:** Anomalous (non-Fickian) transport – combination of diffusion through the gel layer and matrix erosion, typically yielding near-zero-order kinetics when polymer ratio is balanced.

**Tuning Guide:** If release is too slow → increase SSG or reduce ethylcellulose. If too fast → increase HPMC K100M or ethylcellulose content. Minor adjustments (2–5%) will shift the curve to match the exact 15/40/60/85 targets.
