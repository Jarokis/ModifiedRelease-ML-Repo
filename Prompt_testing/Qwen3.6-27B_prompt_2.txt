### Release Mechanism Rationale

- **HPMC K4M** forms a viscous gel upon contact with water, slowing diffusion.
- **Carbopol 940** swells significantly at higher pH (>4–5), providing additional control in intestinal segments.
- The **effervescent pair** (citric acid + NaHCO₃) may provide initial buoyancy or rapid wetting but is limited in quantity to avoid burst release.
- **NaCl** increases medium osmolarity inside the tablet, promoting water influx and modulating erosion/diffusion balance.
- **Avicel** adds mechanical strength without significantly altering release kinetics.

Assuming **monolithic matrix tablet**, compressed at ~10 kN, diameter ~10 mm.
